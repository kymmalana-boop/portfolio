About Me

Practical experience with a learner's mindset.

I’m an aspiring E-commerce Virtual Assistant who understands online selling from a hands-on perspective.

Although I’m new to working professionally as an E-commerce VA, I have personal experience sourcing products from different e-commerce platforms and selling them on another platform.
